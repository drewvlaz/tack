Elm Wingtip Collar Shirt |

Regular price $138

Elm is a formal wingtip collar shirt made for casual wear. Traditionally paired with bow ties and tuxedos, we contrast this formal silhouette with jeans and a t-shirt for an effortless, casual, and undone look. Constructed in soft cotton poplin with a more relaxed, casual drape than traditional shirting.

Color

Size

Details

- Wingtip collar
- Button front closure
- Adjustable button-closure cuffs
- Mother-of-pearl button closures
- Relaxed drape
- Curved front and back shirt hem
- Length made to be worn tucked or untucked
- Ultra-soft cotton poplin

Materials & Care
- 100% Cotton
- Care: Machine wash medium

Size & Fit

Relaxed, straight fit. Take your regular size for the intended aesthetic. Nico is 6'0" and wears a size L.

Sustainability

Constructed in 100% soft cotton poplin.
